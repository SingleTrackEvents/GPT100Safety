Place the point on the parking/access spot. Optionally draw the walking track to the course (click along it, ending on the trail). Drive times can be auto-filled, but for gated roads enter the real time your team can drive. Use “Draw drive route” to trace the actual driving line (avoids weird auto-routing through gates), and “Draw walk track” for the walk to the course.
